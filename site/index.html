Who we are

We started with fifteen trucks and our own cargo. Today we move the region's.

01 — Origin

Martin Hardware began importing tiles and glass into Kigali in 2012. Three years later we stopped waiting on other people's trucks.

02 — Scale

A hundred tractor units and a hundred and two trailers, with ten more of each joining the yard every year.

03 — Reach

Two ocean ports, one landlocked capital, and every province in between — plus onward haulage into the DRC.
